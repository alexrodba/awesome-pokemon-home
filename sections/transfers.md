# Transfer rules & one-way traps

_Last updated: October 2026. See the [changelog](../README.md#changelog) for the full history._

Moving Pokémon around HOME is mostly safe, but a few transfers are **irreversible** or **flat-out blocked**. This page collects the gotchas scattered across the rest of the guide so you can check before you move anything.

## One-way traps (you can't undo these)

| Trap | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Legends: Z-A is a one-way door** | Bring a Pokémon from an older game into Z-A and it **permanently** loses the ability to return to those earlier games. Pokémon caught in Z-A can't be sent back to previous games at all. |
| **FireRed/LeafGreen → HOME is one-way** | Once a 'mon leaves FireRed/LeafGreen for HOME, it can never go back, and 'mons from other games can't enter FireRed/LeafGreen at all. [Details here](games.md#pokemon-firered--leafgreen). |
| **Let's Go → Sword/Shield is one-way** | 'Mons can move between Let's Go Pikachu/Eevee and HOME freely, but once you move one into Sword/Shield it can't return to Let's Go. |
| **Pokémon GO → HOME is one-way** | The GO Transporter only moves 'mons **from GO into HOME**, never back. Think before you transport. |
| **Pokémon Bank → HOME is one-way** | 'Mons moved from Bank into HOME can't go back to Bank. Bank transfers need the Premium plan, and Bank shuts down on February 25th, 2027 (US time), so move anything left there before then. |

## Blocked transfers (they just won't work)

| Limitation | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Spinda & Nincada can't enter BDSP** | You can't bring Spinda or Nincada into Brilliant Diamond / Shining Pearl. [More exceptions here](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/pokemon-home-spinda-nincada-gigantamax-pikachu-eevee/). |
| **Spinda can't leave GO** | Spinda can't be transferred from Pokémon GO into HOME. |
| **FireRed/LeafGreen box restrictions** | Transfers go one whole box at a time, and 'mons with held items, 'mons that know an HM, and Eggs are skipped. Only one of each "Special Pokémon" per save file. |

## Handy facts (these work _in your favour_)

| Fact | Why it's useful |
| --- | --- |
| **Origin sticks after evolving elsewhere** | A Pokémon counts for the dex of the game where it was **caught or hatched**, not where it evolved. So you can catch a Gastly in BDSP, evolve it into Gengar in Legends: Arceus, bring it back, and it still fills the BDSP entry. |
| **Trade-evolutions can skip the trade** | Several 'mons that normally evolve by trading (Gengar, Machamp, Golem, Alakazam and more) can be evolved with an item in Legends: Arceus instead - no second player required. |
| **Some dexes fill without transferring** | Z-A's Mega Evolution Pokédex and your FireRed/LeafGreen Pokédex sync straight from your save, so you don't have to move 'mons out (and lock them out of their game) just to get the entries. |
| **GO Safari Ball 'mons can transfer now** | Since HOME 4.1.0, 'mons caught in a Safari Ball in Pokémon GO can be sent to HOME. They arrive in a Strange Ball. |
